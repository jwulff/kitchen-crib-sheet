## Prep

### 🍋 Lemon Dressing
- Lemon juice, 1T
- Olive oil, 3T
- Salt & pepper
- Shake together

### 🥔 Roast Potatoes
- Potatoes, 500g
- Olive oil, 2T
- Salt
- Roast 425°F, 30–40 min
- Turn halfway; cook until tender

## Dinner

### 🍚 Rice
- Rice, 1c
- Water, 1½c
- Rinse rice
- Covered simmer, 15 min
- Rest off heat, 10 min

### 🧀 Grilled Cheese
- Bread, 2 slices
- Cheddar, 50g
- Butter, 1T
- Toast both sides over medium-low

## Breakfast

### 🥞 Pancakes
- Flour, 1c
- Milk, 1c
- Egg, 1
- Baking powder, 2t
- Butter, 1T melted
- Mix; griddle until cooked through

### 🥣 Overnight Oats
- Oats, ½c
- Milk, ½c
- Yogurt, ¼c
- Refrigerate overnight

## Sweet

### 🍮 Custard Reminder
- Use your usual custard recipe
- Keep the heat gentle
- Strain before chilling

### 🫐 Yogurt Bowl
- Yogurt, 150g
- Berries, 50g
- Granola, 30g
- Assemble just before eating
