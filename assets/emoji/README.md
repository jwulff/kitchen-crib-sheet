# Bundled icons

Twemoji graphics by Twitter, Inc. and other contributors, licensed under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE-GRAPHICS).

Source: [jdecked/twemoji](https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji/tree/b6b55fef1e8636b540a6d016a4729ca8cdf2e60b/assets/72x72), commit `b6b55fef1e8636b540a6d016a4729ca8cdf2e60b`. The PNG assets are unmodified copies. The renderer composites them on white and converts them to grayscale; this adaptation is identified in the generated attribution footer. Keep that attribution and its links when sharing generated sheets with these icons.

Supported title prefixes:

🌶️ 🥩 🥦 🍚 🌿 🥗 🧀 🐟 🦐 🍲 🍳 🍕 🧈 🥣 🍮 🫐 🥞 🍋 🥔

A title may also have no emoji. To add another icon, copy its PNG from the pinned upstream version, retain this notice, and update `manifest.json`. No system font images are redistributed.
